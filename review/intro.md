## Where the dashboard numbers come from

**Current + Historical → six reporting datasets → the reporting dashboard**

| You upload | What it supplies |
| --- | --- |
| **UTI Individual Current** | Individual submissions. Only records marked complete enter the reporting calculations. |
| **UTI Individual Historical** | Earlier individual submissions. Combined with Current; records are not automatically deduplicated. |
| **CSiM Hospitals and States** | Matches hospital codes to names and states. A missing code currently excludes that hospital's records. |
