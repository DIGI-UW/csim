Choose specific reporting dates

The official Superset 6.1.0 dashboard has its filters on the left. Time Period opens directly in Custom, with a start and end date.

Open the dashboard → · Login details

Choose dates, then choose the grouping

  1. Choose your hospital in the left filter panel.
  2. Open Time Period. Enter a start and end using the two Specific Date/Time fields.
  3. Choose Time Unit: Month, Quarter or Year. Apply the filters.

For February through March: start February 1 and end April 1. The start is included; the end is excluded. CSiM reports monthly observations, represented by the first day of each month.

The saved starting window is September 1, 2025 to September 1, 2026. It stays fixed until an editor changes the default.

What the checks cover

The public dashboard opens in Custom immediately, applies changed dates to its reporting summary and chart, and retains those dates when reopened. The known-record example also distinguishes a missing month from zero and confirms that February–March grouped into Q1 does not add January.

All 21 reporting charts and six reporting datasets remain, plus the reporting-period summary. The official Superset application is unchanged.
